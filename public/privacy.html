APPLICANT INFORMATION

Privacy with
purpose.

This operational notice describes the current application portal. NLQI must complete legal and data-protection review before production use.

Information collected

When you create an applicant account, NLQI stores your name, email address, password hash, verification state and account activity. An application may include your contact and biographical details, education, personal profile, written answers, emergency contact, declarations, uploaded documents, application status and payment status.

How it is used and who can see it

Information is used to administer your account, review and score your application, contact you about the programme, manage application fees when payments are enabled, and maintain administrative and security records. NLQI administrators can review submitted application details and documents. Judges can read applications assigned to them and record their scores; they do not receive document-download access through the judge portal.

Storage and service providers

The current application stores account and application records in the server’s SQLite database and stores uploaded documents in a private server directory. SMTP providers process messages when email delivery is configured. Paystack receives transaction details only when NLQI enables checkout. This software does not currently transfer application files to an external file-hosting provider.

Retention and your requests

The application does not currently automatically delete accounts, applications or uploaded documents, and a retention period has not been configured. Contact NLQI to ask about access, correction, deletion or other data requests. NLQI must set and publish a lawful retention schedule and operational request process before accepting production applications.

Before production use

This notice is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. NLQI should identify its data controller and contact details, establish a lawful basis and retention schedule, document processor arrangements, and obtain qualified privacy review before collecting real applicant information.
